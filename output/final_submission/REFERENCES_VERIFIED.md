# Literature citation verification

Bibliographic metadata for the five recent studies in Table 1 was checked on
20 October 2026 against publisher pages (or the authors' publisher-hosted
record). The descriptions in the report are concise summaries of each cited
study's stated scope and reported method; reported results are not transferred
to the present prototype.

| Report reference | Publisher / authoritative record | Verified scope used in the report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [14] de Almeida et al. (2022) | [Elsevier, Expert Systems with Applications](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2022.116731) | Systematic review of vision parking-lot datasets and evaluation protocols; discusses dataset dependence and gaps in diverse conditions. |
| [15] Lee et al. (2022) | [Elsevier, Microprocessors and Microsystems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micpro.2022.104612) | Review of edge computing for secure and energy-efficient smart parking. |
| [16] Feng et al. (2022) | [Springer Nature, Complex & Intelligent Systems](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40747-022-00700-1) | Zone-wise vacant-space prediction using a dual ConvLSTM and dense convolution network; evaluates public-lot data. |
| [17] Zhang et al. (2024) | [MDPI, ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi13050151) | Compares TCN and LSTM, and proposes MAT-LSTM for parking-space availability using real and synthetic datasets. |
| [18] Enríquez et al. (2024) | [MDPI, Applied System Innovation](https://doi.org/10.3390/asi7030052) | Evaluates parking prediction using real parking-flow data and compares fog/cloud response delay using simulation. |

These sources support the literature discussion only. Their data, architecture,
and performance figures are not treated as measurements of this repository.
